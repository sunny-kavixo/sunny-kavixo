## Engineering focus

- **Reliable AI and evaluation:** explicit evidence boundaries, reproducible experiments, and uncertainty-aware decisions
- **Developer tools:** local-first debugging, repair verification, test automation, and inspectable artifacts
- **Data and annotation quality:** deterministic checks with human review rather than unsupported model claims
- **Accessible software:** keyboard-path testing and regression evidence for real user journeys
- **Physical AI and robotics:** telemetry-based evaluation, ROS 2 safety components, and honest simulation/hardware boundaries

## Flagship work

### [ProofCausality AI](https://github.com/sunny-kavixo/proofcausality-ai)

Experimental visual-evidence verification for annotation-quality and physical-action assessment. Release **v0.2.8** records **217 Python tests** and **19 Rust tests**; the repository clearly limits its results to controlled prototype validation rather than independent physical or human-subject validation.

`Python` `FastAPI` `computer vision` `Rust` `Playwright`

### [Sunny DevFix](https://github.com/sunny-kavixo/Sunny-DevFix)

A local-first Python debugging and repair-evidence lab. Its workflow separates a proposed or applied repair from a repair that has been independently verified, and documents approval, path-containment, secret-redaction, and optional container-verification boundaries. Release **v0.2.1** and CI are published; the documented suite reports **86 passing tests**.

`Python` `FastAPI` `agent workflows` `Docker` `security controls`

### [SwitchPath](https://github.com/sunny-kavixo/switchpath)

A controlled accessibility-testing proof of concept that compares equivalent mouse and keyboard booking journeys, reads saved state independently, and generates Playwright regression tests. Recorded verification covers **12 backend tests** and **5 Chromium E2E tests**; screen-reader, complete contrast, and cross-browser verification remain outside its tested scope.

`TypeScript` `React` `Fastify` `Playwright` `accessibility`

### [AI Annotation Quality Control](https://github.com/sunny-kavixo/ai-annotation-quality-control)

An annotation-quality workflow for CSV structure, bounding boxes, image dimensions, taxonomy consistency, reviewer decisions, and evidence export. The implementation uses deterministic checks and human review; it does not claim universal annotation correctness.

`Python` `image QA` `human review` `pytest` `GitHub Actions`

### [Physical AI Robot Task Evaluator](https://github.com/sunny-kavixo/physical-ai-robot-task-evaluator)

A DROID Episode 0 evaluation prototype that combines real recorded telemetry with separately reviewed visual evidence. Its reports distinguish measured episode signals from exploratory detector/tracker analysis and do not present the work as independent physical validation.

`Python` `robotics` `telemetry` `computer vision` `reporting`

### [ROS 2 Autonomous Navigation Safety Robot](https://github.com/sunny-kavixo/ros2-autonomous-navigation-safety-robot)

ROS 2 Jazzy navigation and safety source with successful source/build CI. End-to-end Gazebo, Nav2, and real-hardware validation are explicitly documented as pending.

`C++` `ROS 2 Jazzy` `Nav2` `Gazebo` `CI`

## Engineering principles

- **Evidence over claims:** state what was tested, how it was measured, and what remains unknown.
- **Reproducibility by default:** keep tests, commands, fixtures, and artifacts inspectable.
- **Human accountability:** use automation to support—not obscure—review and responsibility.
- **Secure boundaries:** minimize data exposure and make risky operations explicit.
- **Accessible systems:** treat keyboard and assistive access as engineering requirements.
